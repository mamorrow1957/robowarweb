Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Who we are

RoboWar is a browser-based programming game hosted at robowar.morroweb.com, operated by Michael Morrow. You can reach us at help@morrowlabsmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your chosen username
  • Your email address
  • Your password (stored as a one-way bcrypt hash — we cannot read it)
  • The robot programs you save to your account

We do not collect your real name, location, payment information, or any other personal data. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs.

Why we collect it

  • Username — to identify your account and display your name in the game
  • Email address — to verify your account, let you reset a forgotten password, and to notify you if your account is locked due to suspicious login activity. We will never send you marketing emails.
  • Robot programs — to save your work so you can access it from any device

What we don't do

We will never:

  • Sell or share your information with third parties
  • Use your email address for marketing, newsletters, or any purpose other than account management
  • Use your data to train AI models
  • Share your robot programs publicly without your explicit action (the Share feature is opt-in per robot)

Data retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing help@morrowlabsmail.com. Deleted accounts and their robots are removed immediately and are not recoverable.

Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (10 rounds) and are never stored in plain text. Email addresses are stored securely and never included in server logs. Authentication uses short-lived tokens with server-side revocation on logout.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or account deletion: help@morrowlabsmail.com
